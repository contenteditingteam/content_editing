Our Services

Whatever you write, there is an editor ready to improve it.

All services

🎓 Academic Editing

Journal articles, research papers and scholarly writing. We sharpen the argument, correct the language and make sure the paper follows the conventions of your field.

  • Grammar, wording and sentence flow
  • Consistent academic tone
  • Clear abstract, headings and transitions

📑 Thesis & Dissertation Editing

A long document must read as one piece, from the first chapter to the last. We check language, structure and consistency across the whole thesis.

  • Chapter-to-chapter consistency
  • Heading, table and figure labels
  • Citation and reference style checks

📄 Manuscript Editing

Get a paper ready to send to a journal: a clear message, logical sections and wording that matches the journal's author guidelines.

  • Language polish before submission
  • Reviewer-ready clarity
  • Help with cover letters and responses

🔬 Scientific & Medical Editing

Technical writing needs care. We improve readability while keeping terminology, units and descriptions of methods and data accurate.

  • Terminology and unit consistency
  • Clear methods and results sections
  • Flags for gaps or unclear claims

💼 Business Editing

Memos, reports, white papers, proposals, websites and handbooks. We make your message clear, concise and professional.

  • Plain, confident wording
  • Consistent brand voice
  • Tighter structure and headings

⚡ Same-Day Editing

2-hour, 4-hour and 8-hour turnaround for urgent documents. Word limits apply to each speed.

  • Fast turnaround for short documents
  • Same quality checks
  • Ideal for deadlines and applications

🌐 ESL Editing

For writers whose first language is not English. We fix grammar and word choice and reshape sentences so the writing reads naturally.

  • Natural phrasing and idioms
  • Article, tense and preposition fixes
  • Your meaning stays intact

📚 Book Editing

Fiction and non-fiction, from big-picture feedback on structure and pacing to line-by-line copyediting and a final proofread.

  • Developmental feedback
  • Copyediting and line editing
  • Final proofread before publishing

✏️ Essay Editing

College essays, personal statements and coursework. A clearer thesis, better flow and a voice that is still yours.

  • Stronger opening and conclusion
  • Smoother paragraph links
  • Grammar and punctuation cleanup

🔍 Proofreading

The final pass on a finished document: spelling, grammar, punctuation and formatting slips.

  • Typos and punctuation
  • Formatting consistency
  • Quick and affordable

✍️ Rewriting

If a draft is unclear or hard to follow, our writers restructure and rewrite it while keeping your meaning and voice.

  • Clearer structure
  • Original, natural wording
  • Keeps your key points

🛡️ Plagiarism Check

A similarity screening with a clear report, so you can fix overlap and cite properly before you submit.

  • Similarity report
  • Guidance on rewording and citing
  • Peace of mind before submission

Choose your editing level

Not sure how much help you need? Start here.

Proofreading

A light final pass. We correct spelling, grammar, punctuation and formatting, and leave your wording as it is. Best when the writing is already strong.

Substantive Editing

A deeper edit of structure, flow and clarity. We reorder weak sections, tighten arguments and explain the larger changes in comments.

Scientific & Technical Editing

An editor with subject knowledge reviews terminology, the description of methods and consistency of data, on top of the language edit.
