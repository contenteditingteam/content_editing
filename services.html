Frequently asked questions

What editing services does Content Editing offer?

Content Editing offers academic, thesis and dissertation, manuscript, scientific and medical, business, ESL, book and essay editing, plus proofreading, rewriting, same-day editing and plagiarism checks, all done by human editors.

What is the difference between editing and proofreading?

Editing improves clarity, flow, structure and tone. Proofreading is a final pass that corrects spelling, grammar, punctuation and formatting errors in an already finished document.

Do you edit theses and journal articles?

Yes. Academic, thesis and dissertation editing is one of our main services, and every completed order includes an editing certificate you can submit with your work.

Will the editor change my meaning or research?

No. Editors improve language, clarity and consistency and leave your ideas, data and conclusions unchanged. Larger suggestions are explained in comments so you decide what to accept.

How fast can I get my document back?

Turnaround ranges from a few hours for same-day editing to several days for standard orders. Your delivery time is shown when you upload your document.
