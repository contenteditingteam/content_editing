Frequently asked questions

How much does manuscript editing cost?

Manuscript and academic editing starts from $0.024 per word with a 5-day turnaround. The exact manuscript editing cost depends on your word count and deadline, and is shown before you pay.

What is the thesis editing price?

Thesis and dissertation editing uses the per-word academic editing rate, so the price depends on the length of your thesis and how quickly you need it. Upload your file to see the exact thesis editing price.

What are your proofreading rates?

Proofreading starts from $0.018 per word with a 5-day turnaround, with faster options available at a higher rate. Proofreading cost is lower than full editing because it is a lighter pass.

How much does editing cost?

Content Editing charges per word. Rates start from $0.018 per word for proofreading with a 5-day turnaround and from $0.024 per word for academic editing. Faster turnaround costs more, and same-day editing is priced at a premium.

How is the price of my document calculated?

Your word count is multiplied by the per-word rate for the service and deadline you choose. When you upload your document, the word count is worked out automatically and your exact price and delivery time are shown before you pay.

Can I pay in Indian rupees?

Yes. You can switch between US dollars and Indian rupees on the pricing page and in the instant price calculator on the home page.

What is included in the price?

Every order includes Track Changes and comments, a clean final copy, an editing certificate and private, secure file handling.

Why does a faster turnaround cost more?

A shorter deadline means the editor has to move other work aside, so rush and same-day delivery are priced at a higher per-word rate.
