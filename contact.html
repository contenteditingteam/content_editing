How we can help

Questions about editing or proofreading

Not sure whether you need editing or proofreading, or which service suits a thesis, journal manuscript, essay or business document? Tell us about your document and we will point you to the right option. You can also read our guide to proofreading vs editing.

Pricing and custom quotes

Prices are per word and shown instantly when you upload a file, so most orders do not need a quote. For very large projects or special requirements, choose "Pricing or a custom quote" in the form above. See our pricing page for every rate.

Help with an existing order

If you already have an order, include the document title or order details in your message. You can also message the team directly from the order in your dashboard.

What to include in your message

The type of document, the approximate word count, your deadline and any style or journal guidelines help us reply with a useful answer the first time.
